How it works

Three steps from study time to insight

1

Study the material

Lecturers upload slides, notes, and reading. Students open them straight in the platform, on any device.

2

Your engagement is measured

Attention and comprehension are tracked in real time as you read, scroll, and answer quiz and assigment.

3

Get insight & recommendations

Students get resources for the topics they missed; lecturers and departments see live dashboards of progress.
